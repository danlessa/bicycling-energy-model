A afirmação

All four parameters are recoverable within the pre-stated plausible ranges for all three riders: mass from braking-free sustained climbs is CdA-robust (JAAM's implied 103 kg was genuine — rider-confirmed ≈ 100 ± 7 kg, retiring Entry 14's CdA-misspecification guess); CdA and C_rr become identifiable only once per-activity wind is modelled via a LINEARISED aero regression (flat-power, coast-down and climb-only methods all fail for CdA); the wind vector itself comes from GPS-bearing directional asymmetry, de-biased for regression attenuation.
Conforme registrado no diário de comparação de modelos e no grafo de afirmações (claims.ttl) — a prosa da entrada permanece a fonte autoritativa.
